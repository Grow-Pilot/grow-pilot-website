01 · The reason

Where it came from

Growers ask for this from two sides. One group already rewards on performance and works it out in Excel: export from the path-registration system, add up by hand and then calculate a bonus based on speed. Quality is hard to fit in, so you reward pace and nothing else, or quality by gut feeling. The other group has wanted to start for a while, but dreads setting up something that works out fairly and that you can explain to the workers.

02 · The solution

What it solves

The bonus is calculated on the hours someone actually worked, against a standard you adjust per week to the available work. Holidays and absence automatically don't count. Quality does count and this is fully configurable: anyone below the set score builds up no bonus, regardless of speed. For side tasks without a standard, the owner decides per worker and that choice can always be changed. What comes out is an amount per worker, ready for payroll. The foreman checks, GrowPilot calculates, the owner approves, payroll processes.

What it looks like

Bonus module in the app

The weekly overview: bonus to pay out, what was delivered above the standard and what was withheld on quality. You approve the week, then it goes to payroll.

Per worker: hours, performance above the bonus standard and the quality score. Anyone below the quality cap builds up no bonus, however fast they work.

You set the bonus standard per activity, per week and per site. GrowPilot suggests one based on last week.

Tiers, groups, quality cap and recheck limit. The worked example recalculates on the spot: what the bonus costs and what working faster delivers.

Cost per unit: what a kilo costs at the standard without bonus and with bonus. Green means the bonus makes the unit cheaper.
