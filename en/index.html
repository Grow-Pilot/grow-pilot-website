The problem

Your greenhouse isn't running on one system

Verbal instructions, paper or gut feeling: there is no system that holds everyone to the same standard and shows how the greenhouse is really doing right now. The result: direction comes too late, quality and speed drop.

“You're giving loose instructions everywhere at once.”
Sound familiar?

Owner

No grip on what's happening. Loss of speed, quality and margin.

Foreman

Putting out fires instead of leading. No backbone, all the responsibility.

Workers

Unclear expectations. Inconsistent judgement, arbitrary calls.
The solution

How GrowPilot works

You set the standard per crop. GrowPilot makes quality and speed visible every day and corrects right away.

Integrations

Works with your system

GrowPilot doesn't replace your current path registration, it connects to it. These are the three most-used systems in greenhouse horticulture. No manual entry, no extra systems. Your data instantly visible, with more functionality to steer better.

Using a different path-registration system? Get in touch, we'd be happy to explore the possibilities.
