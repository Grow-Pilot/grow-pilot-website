About us

Built from inside the greenhouse

When your business grows, you can't be everywhere at once. Lowie Verbeek noticed that standards were on paper or in a system, but that daily execution depended on who was walking around and how busy it was.

Systems that were hard to configure. Random checks. Ad-hoc corrections. Quality and speed that slipped as soon as attention faded. GrowPilot was built to change that. Not reacting after the fact, but directing proactively, even when you're not there yourself.
