The process

Live in your greenhouse within a week

The technical part is sorted quickly. After that we make sure together that GrowPilot runs the way you work.

01right away

Connection

As soon as we start working together we connect to your path-registration system and check that all data comes in correctly.

02together

Setup

We prepare the checkpoints, lists and standards based on your way of working. You review, supply input and do the necessary groundwork.

03half a day on site

Implementation morning

We go through the checkpoints, frequencies and all other settings and put the app on the foremen's phones. Checks start that same day.

04weekly

Coaching

In the first month we evaluate weekly: what works and where we adjust. What you bring in is often back in the platform within two weeks. After that we agree on the evaluation interval you prefer.

Changes you make are visible on the work floor right away
