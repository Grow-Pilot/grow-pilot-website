The process

Live in your greenhouse within a week

The technical part is quickly arranged. After that we make sure GrowPilot runs the way you work.

01

Connection

right away

As soon as we start working together we connect your path registration system and check that all data comes in correctly.

02

Setup

We prepare the check points, lists and standards based on your way of working. You look over our shoulder, supply what we need and do the groundwork.

03

Implementation morning

half a day on site

We go through the check points, the frequencies and all other settings, and put the app on the foremen's phones. Checking starts that same afternoon.

04

Support

weekly

In the first month we evaluate every week: what works and where we adjust. What you raise often shows up in the platform within two weeks. After that we agree together on the right interval.
